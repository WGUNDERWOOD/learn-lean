** Basics of type-theoretic logic (tablet)
*** Curry-Howard isomorphism
*** Proposition -> Type
*** Proof of P -> Element of P
*** We write n : N, N : Type (the universe of types)
*** Similarly, rfl : (1 + 1), (1 + 1) : Prop
*** trivial : True, True : Prop
*** The proposition False : Prop has no proofs!
*** If P, Q : Prop, then a proof of "P -> Q" is a function
**** It maps proofs of P to proofs of Q
*** A proof of "P and Q" is a pair of proofs, one for P and one for Q
*** A proof of "not P" is a function
**** It maps proofs of P to proofs of False
**** Because "not P" is "P implies False"
*** "P or Q" is more complicated
